ASU CSE180 Discussion Board Etiquette

These are general guidelines for our course discussion board and for online forums more broadly. Students who follow good discussion habits make the forum easier to read, easier to use, and more helpful for everyone. We reserve the right to remove posts that break these rules.

  1. Post new threads in the correct forum.
  2. Do not post solutions to assignments or quizzes.
  3. Check before you ask. Before starting a new thread, make sure the question has not already been answered. Read the assignment and course materials carefully, then scan existing threads.
  4. Use the Search button. Search the discussion board to see whether someone else has already asked the same question.
  5. Write a clear subject line. Make your title descriptive so readers can quickly tell what your post is about. For example, use Cannot log in to Secure Shell instead of problem or help.
  6. Keep the message focused. The body of your post should be direct and to the point. Include only relevant details.
  7. Do not write in ALL CAPS. It can feel like shouting.
  8. Use proper grammar and spelling. Everything you publish on the web reflects on you, so take a moment to proofread.
  9. Use formatting sparingly. Bold, italics, colors, and unusual fonts should be used only when they truly improve readability. Excessive punctuation and decorative formatting can be distracting.
    “Just because you can, does not mean you should.”
  10. Be polite and considerate. Treat faculty, staff, and classmates with respect. If you are joking, make it obvious, because written comments are easy to misread. A quick reread before posting is always a good idea.
  11. Feel free to answer questions. This is a group forum where students can help one another. Just remember not to give away solutions. We monitor the discussion boards and read what is posted, but we are not available 24/7. In the meantime, students often do an excellent job helping each other.
In short: post clearly, stay respectful, and help build a discussion board that is useful for everyone.

Thank you for keeping the discussion board professional, friendly, and helpful. - by Sherry Feng @ASU
