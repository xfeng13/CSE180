Online Course Readiness Self Assessment

How well will this online course fit your circumstances and life style? Write down your answer to each question as A, B or C, then scroll to the end to see the result and analysis.

Students who tend to be most successful are those individuals who are self-directed and self-motivated in their learning and study habits. Answer honestly since no one will see the result but you! (*This survey is partly adapted from "Are Telecourses for Me?" from PBS-Adult Learning Service)

Question 1

B. Moderate. I could take it on campus later or substitute another course.
C. Low. It could be postponed or retake.
.

Self Assessment Result Analysis

Add 3 points for each "A" that you selected, 2 for each "B" and 1 for each "C".
 

  • If you scored 40 or more, the online course is a real possibility for you.
  • If you scored between 30 and 39, the online courses may work for you, but you may need to make a few adjustments in your schedule and study habits to succeed.
  • If you scored 29 or less, this online course currently may not be the best alternative for you.

Of course, nothing is carved in stone, and even if you scored a low number on this survey and you could still succeed in this course. The purpose of this survey is to familiarize yourself with the traits of successful online course learners; however, not all distance learners fit one profile, so use your own judgment, and if you are determined, then try your best at it and see how it goes
