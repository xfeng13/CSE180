Six Tips for Success in CSE180

Sherry Feng

CSE180 is a fully online and accelerated course. The six tips below will help you stay organized, keep up with the weekly modules, and get the most from the course.

1

Be Prepared Before the Course Begins

Before the class begins, make sure you have adequate computer hardware and software and that your system meets the technology requirements. Make sure you have a regular place for study and a planned routine for completing assignments.

2

Understand How CSE180 Is Structured

The course is organized into weekly folders. During the Fall and Spring semesters, each weekly folder contains two modules. During the Summer semester, each weekly folder contains three modules because of the accelerated schedule.

Unless otherwise noted, all modules are due either Tuesday or Thursday at 11:59 PM (Arizona time) for Fall & Spring semesters; and Monday, Wednesday, Friday for Summer semesters. Log in to Canvas at the beginning of the semester, review the course schedule, and either print or save a copy for future reference. Follow the schedule closely and complete each module by its due date.

3

Set Aside Time to Study

Online courses take just as much study time as face-to-face courses. Because CSE180 is a 7.5-week course in Fall & Spring and only 6.5-week in Summer, it is much more concentrated and accelerated than a regular 15-week course. Be prepared to spend at least 3–4 hours per day on the weekly coursework and submissions.

4

Build Self-Motivation and Good Time Management

Since this is a fully online course with no scheduled class meetings, you are free to work at your own pace, anytime and anywhere you have a reliable Internet connection.

Each weekly folder is published at least two weeks before its due date, so you are encouraged to work ahead whenever possible. This flexibility also requires self-discipline and good time management. It is your responsibility to complete the required coursework and submit all assignments by their deadlines.

To stay on track, set aside dedicated time each day to work on the course and complete your assignments on time.

5

Read Carefully

Most of the course materials are provided in written form. Therefore, it is essential that you carefully read the course instructions and all assigned materials.

You are also expected to check the course announcements on Canvas every day, as important updates, reminders, and clarifications will be posted throughout the semester.

6

Communication Is Key

Because this is an online course, the instructor and TAs cannot see when you have questions as they might in a traditional classroom. Communicate with us whenever you have questions or encounter difficulties.

Please post course-related questions on the Canvas Discussion Board whenever possible so that other students can also benefit from the answers. If your question involves personal or private matters, feel free to contact us by email instead.

Don't hesitate to ask for help—we are here to support you. Staying in regular communication with the instructional team is one of the keys to your success in this course.

Remember: Stay organized, follow the weekly schedule, read the instructions carefully, and ask for help when you need it.
