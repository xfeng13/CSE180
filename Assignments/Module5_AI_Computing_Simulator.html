CSE180 AI Computing Simulator

Students explore how hardware affects AI performance by running two scenarios: helping one student and helping an entire university. The simulator shows why CPUs are good for small sequential tasks and why GPUs are essential for many simultaneous AI requests.

Scenario 1: Helping One Student

An AI assistant answers a single student's question. The work is mostly sequential, so a CPU can handle it efficiently.

  • One request at a time.
  • Little opportunity for massive parallelism.

Scenario 2: Helping an Entire University

An AI assistant answers questions from thousands of students at the same time. This is a highly parallel workload, which favors GPUs.

  • Many requests at once.
  • GPU cores can process parallel work efficiently.
